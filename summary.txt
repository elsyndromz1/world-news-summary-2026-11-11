Wednesday, 11 November 2026

Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting the 42-day period without new infections needed for an Ebola-free declaration. Nine cases, including two deaths, were recorded in the country, the Associated Press and Reuters reported.

Russian, Ukrainian and U.S. officials began a second day of peace talks in Abu Dhabi, Reuters reported. The Associated Press said the agenda included humanitarian issues and security guarantees; neither outlet reported an immediate breakthrough.

World leaders began formal COP31 discussions in Antalya, Turkey, as nearly 200 countries negotiated, Reuters reported. The Associated Press said UN Secretary-General António Guterres urged stronger action amid a widening gap between climate plans and implementation.

Spain’s first jointly backed general strike in 14 years began on Wednesday, Reuters reported, as unions protested housing costs. The central bank estimates a housing deficit of about 750,000 homes, Reuters said; turnout figures cited in its report came from unions.

Iranian security chief Mohsen Rezaei said Iran would not allow enriched nuclear material to leave the country and warned of a response to new U.S. strikes, Reuters reported. RFE/RL independently reported that excerpts of his televised remarks were released on Wednesday.

Indian shares climbed to their highest levels since February 27, The Hindu reported; at 10:07 a.m. IST, the Nifty was up 1.25% at 25,358 and the Sensex up 1.47% at 82,301.1. Reuters reported foreign investors had bought $5.68 billion of Indian shares in November and cited softer crude prices as support.
